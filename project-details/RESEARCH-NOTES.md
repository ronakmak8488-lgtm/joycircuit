# Reference research notes

Reviewed on 20 July 2026:

- [Poki](https://poki.com/) uses a low-chrome, search-led entry and a dense image-first catalog. Its strongest lesson is minimizing the distance between intent and play.
- [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/) uses an app-like dark shell, persistent navigation, recent play, and themed shelves. Its strongest lesson is giving a large catalog useful structure.
- [Poki product-design case study](https://www.behance.net/gallery/84316615/Poki-brand-strategy-product-design-identity-ux) provides historical brand and product context.

JoyCircuit keeps the useful product principles—fast search, clear categories, recent play, curated discovery, hover/focus feedback, and instant launch—while using an original brand, circuit-path mark, mint/violet/coral palette, featured-game stage, Quick Play panel, and editorial Fresh Drops mosaic.

These sources are references only. Do not copy their assets, page structure, text, game metadata, or code.
